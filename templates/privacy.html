Privacy

Liturgical Calendar for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Liturgical Calendar does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything it shows is computed on your device. When you ask to preview a Scripture passage, the app requests that passage from a Bible text service and shows it; the request contains only the reference. Settings are kept on your device.
