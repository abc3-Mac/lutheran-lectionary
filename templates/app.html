Liturgical Calendar for iPhone, iPad and Mac

The church year, its readings and its propers, in an app that works without a connection.

The same calendar this site has kept since 1997 now runs as a native app. It shows the Sunday or festival for any date from 1583 to 2300, its liturgical color and season, and its readings, in both the Lutheran Service Book three-year series and the historic one-year series.

Everything is computed on the device, so the app works on a plane, in a sacristy without signal, or at a desk years from now. It keeps nothing about you and has no account.

The app is being prepared for the App Store. This page will carry the download link when it is available.

Today, and any day

The day card gives the name, color, season and church year, the appointed readings, the Hymn of the Day, and the LSB Daily Lectionary. In the one-year series it adds the historic introit, collect and gradual. Tap a reading to preview its text. Each weekday of Lent has its own card, with the historic propers.

On iPad and Mac

The larger screens show the calendar as a full table, the one-year propers for every Sunday and festival, the Daily Lectionary chart, Historic Lent, and the Almanac: moon phases, the date of Easter in the West and the East, and the Paschal full moon back to the Exodus.

A widget for the day

Put today's Sunday or festival on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen or the Mac desktop, in its liturgical color. The larger sizes add the readings, the Hymn of the Day and the Daily Lectionary. It turns over at midnight.

Print and share

PDF sheets for a day (card or plain), the church-year calendar, the one-year propers, the Daily Lectionary and each Lenten weekday — the same sheets this site makes. The wheel as a PNG or SVG poster. An iCal file for any year, or a calendar subscription. Print from the Mac, iPhone or iPad.

For planners and scripts

Shortcuts actions on every device. On the Mac, AppleScript commands and an optional local MCP server, so an AI assistant on your own computer can look up the church year and make the PDFs, calendars and wheels.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app goes online only to show a Scripture passage you ask to preview, and nothing is stored.

The app follows this site exactly: its readings, propers and calendars are checked against the website for every year it covers. For sources and permissions, see About; for privacy, see Privacy.
